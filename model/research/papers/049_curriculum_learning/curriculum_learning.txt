Abstract. We show a correspondence between the set of all G-
invariant projectively ﬂat connections on a homogeneous space
M = G/K, and the one of all eG-invariant ﬂat connections on
homogeneous spaces f
M = eG/K, where eG is a central extension of
G (Theorem 3.3).
1. Introduction and statement of results.
Flat connections and projectively ﬂat ones have been extensively
studied by many authors (for examples, [1], [2], [3], [6], [8], [9], [11],
[13]). Even though they are of course very diﬀerent objects, but it
seems that there would exist deep unknown relations between them
each other. In this paper, we want to show some relation between the
set of all G-invariant projectively ﬂat connections on a homogeneous
space M = G/K, and the one of all eG-invariant ﬂat connections on
homogeneous spaces f
M = eG/K, where eG is a central extension of G.

g ⊃k, where eg = g ⊕RE, and [E, eg] = {0}. Let us consider two
